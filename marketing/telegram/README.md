# Telegram marketing templates — Vanta

Ready-to-post templates you can copy, fill in the `{{placeholders}}`, and post
manually. Replace `{{LINK}}` with your site URL and `{{BRAND}}` if you rename
from "Vanta".

## Use these honestly — it protects you

Crypto/gambling channels get banned fast, and deceptive promo is both the
fastest route to a ban *and* a legal liability. Every template here is written
to be truthful on purpose:

- **No "guaranteed wins", "risk-free", or income promises.** They're false, and
  platforms/regulators treat them as fraud signals.
- **Lead with what's actually true and differentiating:** provably fair, a
  disclosed house edge, open verification.
- **Always keep the 18+ / responsible-gambling line.** Don't delete it to save
  space — it's what separates a legitimate operator from a scam channel.
- **Know your jurisdiction.** Many countries/app stores ban gambling promo
  outright. That's on you to check before posting.

## Files

## Formatting notes

Telegram supports **Markdown** and HTML in posts. These use plain Markdown
(`*bold*`, `_italic_`). Emoji are fine and help skimming — don't overdo it.
Keep links to one clear CTA per post.
