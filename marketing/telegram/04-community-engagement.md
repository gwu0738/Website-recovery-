# Community / engagement posts

Low-effort posts to keep the room active between announcements. An active channel
converts far better than a dead one. Don't fake testimonials or winnings —
screenshots of *verifiable* results are fine; invented ones are fraud.

---

## Questions / polls
- "Riskier or safer bettor? 🎲 Dice low-target or 🚀 Limbo moonshots?"
- "Which game should we spotlight next? Drop it below 👇"
- "What's your client seed strategy — random every time, or a lucky phrase?"

## Verification challenges (great for trust + engagement)
- "Verify any bet you made today and post the ✅ screenshot. We'll feature a few."
- "Think you caught a bad roll? Paste the seeds, let's verify it together, live."

## Feature / roadmap updates
- "Crash is coming next. What cash-out features do you want? Auto-cashout?
  One-tap? Tell us."
- "You asked, we're building: {{FEATURE}}. ETA {{DATE}}."

## Transparency posts (lean into these — they're your edge)
- "Reminder: our house edge is {{EDGE}}%. Stated, fixed, verifiable. No tricks."
- "Our fairness code is open — read exactly how every result is generated:
  {{LINK}}"

## Responsible-gambling posts (post these regularly, not just for show)
- "Set a budget before you play. The house always has an edge — that's math, not
  opinion. Play for fun, stop when it stops being fun. 💜"
- "Chasing losses is how the fun ends. If gambling stops feeling like a game,
  take a break. Help: begambleaware.org"

---

_Keep a pinned message with: age limit (18+), responsible-gambling resources,
and a line that results are verifiable. It sets the tone for the whole channel._
