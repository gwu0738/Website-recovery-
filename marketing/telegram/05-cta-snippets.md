# CTA snippets + reusable blocks

Drop-in lines for the end of any post. Copy, fill placeholders, paste.

## One-line CTAs
- `▶️ Play now: {{LINK}}`
- `🎲 Try it (and verify it): {{LINK}}`
- `💬 Questions? Ask right here in the chat.`
- `🔎 Don't trust us — verify us: {{LINK}}`

## Link block (put at the bottom of bigger posts)
```
🔗 Play: {{LINK}}
💬 Telegram: {{TELEGRAM}}
🎮 Discord: {{DISCORD}}
🐦 X: {{TWITTER}}
```

## Standard responsible-gambling footer (keep on promo posts)
```
18+. Gambling involves risk — never wager more than you can afford to lose.
Online gambling is restricted or illegal in some regions; know your local laws.
```

## Placeholder key
| Placeholder | Replace with |
|-------------|--------------|
| `{{BRAND}}` | your site name (default: Vanta) |
| `{{LINK}}` | your live site URL |
| `{{EDGE}}` | house edge % (default: 1.0) |
| `{{TELEGRAM}}` / `{{DISCORD}}` / `{{TWITTER}}` | your social URLs |
| `{{FEATURE}}` / `{{DATE}}` | whatever you're teasing |

## Don't-do list (gets channels banned / you in legal trouble)
- ❌ "Guaranteed", "risk-free", "can't lose", "free money"
- ❌ Fake winner screenshots or invented testimonials
- ❌ Promising specific returns or income
- ❌ Targeting minors or people with gambling problems
- ❌ Hiding that there's a house edge
